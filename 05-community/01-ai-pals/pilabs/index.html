    Pi Labs (withpi.ai)

    Quick Links
    - Pi Labs Website · Blog · Docs / SDK · Pricing · Support · Discord

    Overview

    Pi Labs provides a fast, deterministic scoring and ranking system powered by foundation models. These are optimized for evaluating LLM outputs across multiple dimensions—like relevance, correctness, style, and user alignment—at low latency (under 100ms) and cost.
    Back to Top

    Core Products & Tools

    • Pi Scorer – A foundation model for nuanced scoring across many dimensions in real-time.
    • Pi Ranking – Customizable cross-encoder ranking models.
    • Pi Embedding – Embeddings geared for retrieval tasks.
    • Applications:
    • Pi Copilot – Assist in tuning scoring behaviors via conversational UX.
    • Spreadsheets & RAG workflows – Integrate into your tables and retrieval chains.
    • LLM Evals – Standardized, embedded evaluation instrumentation.
    • Observability – Transparent runtime monitoring of scoring outputs.
      Back to Top

    Differentiators

    • Fast & lightweight (scoring 20+ dimensions under 100ms).
    • Principle-driven scoring—built to understand intent and quality, not replicate content.
    • Adaptive calibration—align with human preferences or user feedback continuously.
    • Cost-efficient —delivers performance comparable to GPT 4.1 but much cheaper.
