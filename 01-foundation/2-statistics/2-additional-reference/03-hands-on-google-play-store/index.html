    1. Overview

    Advertising is a major driver of app monetization strategies.
    With Android holding ~69% global market share, the Google Play Store is the key platform for distributing mobile applications.

    This case study explores Google Play Store app data to: - Understand category trends - Identify profitable ad placement opportunities - Optimize targeting for an ad agency

    2. Problem Statement

    Context:
    Zoom Ads is an advertising agency aiming to identify trending applications in the Google Play Store.
    They want to focus ad promotion efforts on apps that: - Are growing in popularity - Attract high engagement - Offer maximum ROI for ad spend

    Objective:
    Analyze Google Play Store app data to: - Perform univariate and bivariate analysis - Explore category-level and feature-based patterns - Provide actionable insights for ad targeting

    3. Dataset Description

    The dataset contains detailed app-level information.

    Feature Description
    App Application Name
    Category App category (e.g., Tools, Games)
    Rating Overall user rating
    Reviews Number of user reviews
    Size App size (in KB)
    Installs Number of downloads/installs
    Price App price in USD
    Paid/Free Paid or free app
    Content Rating Target age group
    Ad Supported Whether the app supports ads
    In App Purchases Whether the app has in-app purchases
    Editors Choice Whether the app is an Editor’s Choice

    5. Python Implementation

    import pandas as pd
import matplotlib.pyplot as plt
import seaborn as sns

# Load dataset
df = pd.read_csv("doses.csv")

# Overview
print(df.head())

# Example: Distribution of ratings
plt.figure(figsize=(8,5))
sns.histplot(df['Rating'], bins=20, kde=True, color='blue')
plt.title('Distribution of App Ratings')
plt.xlabel('Rating')
plt.ylabel('Count')
plt.show()

# Category counts
plt.figure(figsize=(10,6))
df['Category'].value_counts().head(10).plot(kind='bar', color='orange')
plt.title('Top 10 App Categories')
plt.ylabel('Number of Apps')
plt.show()

# Correlation heatmap
plt.figure(figsize=(8,6))
sns.heatmap(df[['Rating','Reviews','Installs']].corr(), annot=True, cmap='coolwarm')
plt.title('Correlation Matrix')
plt.show()

    6. Insights & Interpretation

    • Category Trends:
      Tools & Communication dominate in count; Games & Social lead in installs.

    • Paid vs. Free:
      90%+ apps are free, but paid apps in niche categories have loyal users.

    • Ads & Ratings:
      Ad-heavy apps tend to score lower ratings.

    • In-App Purchases:
      High revenue potential in gaming & lifestyle apps with in-app purchases.

    • Size vs. Installs:
      No direct link — small utility apps can still achieve high install counts.

    7. Use Case Impact

    Findings help Zoom Ads: - Focus ads on Gaming, Social, Tools - Prefer free apps with high installs & ratings - Avoid low-rated ad-heavy apps - Consider apps with in-app purchases for premium ad partnerships
