    1. Overview

    Understanding customer personality and behavior is pivotal for businesses to enhance customer satisfaction and increase revenue. Segmentation based on a customer's personality, demographics, and purchasing behavior allows companies to create tailored marketing campaigns, improve customer retention, and optimize product offerings.

    A leading retail company with a rapidly growing customer base seeks to gain deeper insights into their customers' profiles. By understanding customer personalities, lifestyles, and purchasing habits, the company can personalize marketing strategies and create loyalty programs to maintain a competitive edge.

    Objectives: - Develop personalized marketing campaigns to increase conversion rates. - Create effective retention strategies for high-value customers. - Optimize resource allocation such as inventory, pricing, and store layouts.

    2. Dataset Description

    The dataset includes historical data on customer demographics, personality traits, and purchasing behaviors.

    Customer Information

    • ID: Unique identifier for each customer.
    • Year_Birth: Customer's year of birth.
    • Education: Education level.
    • Marital_Status: Marital status.
    • Income: Yearly household income (USD).
    • Kidhome: Number of children.
    • Teenhome: Number of teenagers.
    • Dt_Customer: Date when the customer enrolled.
    • Recency: Days since last purchase.
    • Complain: Customer complained in last 2 years (1 = Yes, 0 = No).

    Spending Information (Last 2 Years)

    • MntWines: Amount spent on wine.
    • MntFruits: Amount spent on fruits.
    • MntMeatProducts: Amount spent on meat.
    • MntFishProducts: Amount spent on fish.
    • MntSweetProducts: Amount spent on sweets.
    • MntGoldProds: Amount spent on gold products.

    Purchase and Campaign Interaction

    • NumDealsPurchases: Purchases made using discounts.
    • AcceptedCmp1 … AcceptedCmp5: Campaign responses (1 = Yes, 0 = No).
    • Response: Response to the last campaign.

    Shopping Behavior

    • NumWebPurchases: Purchases via website.
    • NumCatalogPurchases: Purchases via catalog.
    • NumStorePurchases: Purchases in store.
    • NumWebVisitsMonth: Website visits in last month.

    4. Python Implementation

    # Example: K-Means clustering and visualization
from sklearn.cluster import KMeans
from sklearn.preprocessing import StandardScaler
import pandas as pd

# Load data
df = pd.read_csv("customer_data.csv")

# Feature selection & scaling
features = df.drop(columns=["ID", "Dt_Customer"])
scaler = StandardScaler()
X_scaled = scaler.fit_transform(features)

# K-Means clustering
kmeans = KMeans(n_clusters=3, random_state=42)
df["Cluster"] = kmeans.fit_predict(X_scaled)

# Cluster profiling
cluster_means = df.groupby("Cluster").mean()
print(cluster_means)

    5. Insights & Interpretation

    • Cluster 0: High-income, high-spending customers — ideal for premium offerings and loyalty programs.
    • Cluster 1: Moderate-income, average engagement — focus on cross-selling and targeted promotions.
    • Cluster 2: Low-income, low engagement — re-engagement campaigns and budget-friendly bundles.

    6. Use Case Impact

    Implementing this segmentation strategy can help: - Improve marketing ROI by targeting the right audience. - Increase retention rates through tailored offers. - Optimize inventory and resource allocation.
